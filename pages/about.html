ABOUT INTEL CLUSTERS

About InTeL Clusters.

A proposed information-technology organisation connecting classical digital information, quantum information and practical human-centred technology solutions.

Executive overview

InTeL Clusters focuses on identifying and solving problems across the complete human information environment. Its organising architecture starts with bits and qubits, then connects those foundations to technologies, intelligent systems, institutions and human decision-making.

Core proposition

InTeL connects information from its foundational representation to its movement, protection, expression and practical application—across classical bits and quantum qubits.

What the organisation is designed to address

Information friction includes delays, errors, incompatibility, duplication, information loss, security weaknesses, inaccessible knowledge and barriers between people, systems and institutions.

  • Design and integrate digital technologies and information systems.
  • Develop responsible AI-enabled products and services.
  • Build credible research and partnership pathways for quantum technologies.
  • Apply four common pillars across twenty-six complementary technology domains.
