DEVELOPMENT ROADMAP

Build responsibly.
Prove. Integrate. Scale.

A proposed phased development pathway from a dependable digital core to validated quantum pilots and continuous assurance.

PHASE 01

Phase 1 — Establish the digital core

Define the organisation, governance, technical standards and priority use cases. Build V1 prototypes in selected domains using reliable digital infrastructure and existing AI. Establish minimum controls for all four pillars.

PHASE 02

Phase 2 — Connect the ecosystem

Develop shared identity, data governance, APIs, cybersecurity, cloud and interoperability foundations. Implement transfer, security, manifestation and utilization patterns across pilot services. Publish measurable performance results.

PHASE 03

Phase 3 — Quantum readiness and pilots

Identify narrow, evidence-supported use cases for quantum sensing, communications or computing. Partner with universities and specialist laboratories; compare quantum approaches against strong classical baselines. Evaluate quantum security assumptions and post-quantum migration needs.

PHASE 04

Phase 4 — Advanced intelligence research

Research more capable AI systems with evaluation, security, oversight and governance. Treat AGI as uncertain and ASI as speculative; do not make deployment commitments without demonstrated safety and legitimacy.

PHASE 05

Phase 5 — Scale and continuous assurance

Expand only solutions that demonstrate public or commercial value. Maintain independent security reviews, impact assessments, service monitoring, pillar-level performance measurement and user feedback.

Stage gates

Progression should depend on evidence: a defined problem, approved architecture, working prototype, security and privacy review, performance benchmark, documented operating ownership and demonstrated value. Quantum pilots must include a strong classical comparison and transparent reporting of experimental limits.
