FOUNDATIONAL PILLAR • BIT & QUBIT LEVEL

Information
Transfer.

Detailed bit-level and qubit-level scope, business outcomes, controls and integration responsibilities for this foundational pillar.

Purpose

Move information reliably between sources, destinations, processes, devices, people and systems.

Bit-level scope

data encoding and decoding, packetisation, addressing, routing, switching, network protocols, synchronization, error detection and correction, storage exchange, APIs and interoperability.

Qubit-level scope

preparation and controlled transmission of quantum states, quantum communication channels, entanglement distribution where supported, measurement-aware protocols and classical coordination channels.

Business outcomes

connectivity, interoperability, reduced latency and loss, dependable exchange and access to information across locations and platforms.

Controls and assurance

end-to-end integrity checks, authenticated endpoints, resilient routing, protocol validation, monitoring and recovery procedures.

How this pillar connects to the other three

Transfer makes information available; Security protects it throughout its lifecycle; Manifestation makes it intelligible or expressible; Utilization turns it into value. The pillars may overlap, repeat or operate in parallel. The outputs of one stage become inputs to another.

Technology pathwayHow this pillar is applied
V1 — Digital InTeLConventional digital infrastructure, software, networks and established controls.
V2 — Digital–Quantum InTeLClassical systems integrated with selected quantum components, with explicit interfaces and validated assumptions.
V3 — Quantum InTeLQuantum-first research and engineering where scientifically appropriate, with classical coordination and support.
