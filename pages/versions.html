DIGITAL • HYBRID • QUANTUM

Three technology versions.

Three progressive implementation pathways for every InTeL domain: digital-first, digital–quantum hybrid and quantum-first where scientifically appropriate.

Version 1 — Digital InTeL

Digital technologies + AI + an AGI-ready architecture + a future-oriented ASI research horizon. Uses conventional computing, networks, software, data systems and currently available AI. It provides deployable foundations with modular interfaces that could accommodate more general intelligent systems if and when they become safe and viable. All four pillars are delivered through mature digital engineering practices.

Version 2 — Digital–Quantum InTeL

Digital technologies + quantum technologies + AI + an AGI-ready architecture + a future-oriented ASI research horizon. Combines classical digital infrastructure with selected quantum devices, quantum communications or quantum computing where they offer a demonstrable advantage. Classical systems remain essential for control, user interfaces, error handling, data exchange, security management and most workloads. The four pillars operate across both classical and quantum components.

Version 3 — Quantum InTeL

Quantum technologies + AI + an AGI-ready architecture + a future-oriented ASI research horizon. A quantum-first research and development pathway for problems where quantum information processing, sensing or communication may be useful. It still requires classical control and supporting infrastructure; it is not a claim that all computing or information transfer should become quantum.

Selection principle

Technology choice is evidence-led. Compare maturity, capability, cost, safety and risk. Not every domain requires a quantum implementation; quantum advantage must be demonstrated against strong classical alternatives.
