01 — THE ORGANISATION

Information is the foundation.
Connection is the mission.

InTeL Clusters is a proposed information-technology organisation focused on identifying and solving problems across the complete human information environment. Its central idea is to work from digital information—bits—and quantum information—qubits, then connect those foundations to practical technologies, intelligent systems, institutions and human decision-making.

The A–Z InTeL framework comprises twenty-six complementary domains. Version 2.0 aligns every domain with four foundational pillars that guide the full information lifecycle.

OUR VISION“No friction for information transfers.”

Classical and quantum information are related but distinct engineering domains.

0 1 0 1 1 0

Bits

QUANTUM INFORMATION
|ψ⟩ ⊗ |φ⟩

Qubits

Quantum-information units that can occupy superposition and participate in entanglement.

Explore the foundation →
Scientific design principle

Qubits are not simply faster bits. Quantum advantage is task-specific, measurement is consequential, unknown quantum states cannot be perfectly cloned, and practical systems face noise and decoherence.

05 — A–Z TECHNOLOGY ECOSYSTEM

Twenty-six domains.
One connected purpose.

Each domain is aligned to the four pillars and the three technology versions where appropriate.

A

Adaptive Intelligence & Automation

Builds adaptive software agents, workflow automation and decision-support tools. It aims to reduce repetitive work, improve responsiveness and help people act on reliable information.

B

Blockchain, Identity & Trust

Develops verifiable digital identity, provenance, tamper-evident records and trusted transactions. It addresses fraud, weak audit trails and uncertainty about who created or changed information.

C

Communication & Connectivity

Improves the movement of information across networks, devices and communities through interoperable communications, resilient connectivity and efficient protocols.

D

Data Intelligence & Engineering

Collects, cleans, organises, analyses and governs data so that information is accurate, discoverable and useful for research, operations and decisions.

E

Education & Knowledge Systems

Creates learning platforms, digital libraries, personalized learning support and knowledge-sharing systems to reduce barriers to education and lifelong learning.

F

Finance & Economic Systems

Supports secure payments, financial analytics, fraud detection, digital accounting and transparent economic information for individuals, businesses and institutions.

G

Governance, Law & Public Services

Enables accessible public information, digital service delivery, records management, policy analysis and accountable administration while respecting rights and due process.

H

Health & Human Wellbeing

Supports health information systems, clinical workflow tools, medical research, remote care and public-health analytics, with privacy, clinical oversight and safety controls.

I

Internet, Web & Interoperability

Connects websites, applications, APIs, platforms and digital services so systems can exchange information consistently and users can navigate services with less friction.

J

Justice, Safety & Cybersecurity

Strengthens cyber defence, digital forensics, incident response, risk management and secure justice-related information systems under lawful authority and human oversight.

K

Knowledge Representation & Discovery

Organises knowledge using semantic systems, knowledge graphs, search and reasoning tools so relationships, evidence and expertise can be found and understood.

L

Logistics, Supply Chains & Mobility

Improves tracking, routing, inventory, transport coordination and supply-chain visibility to reduce delays, waste, uncertainty and service interruptions.

M

Manufacturing, Materials & Industry

Applies automation, digital twins, industrial analytics and materials research to improve production quality, equipment reliability and resource efficiency.

N

Networks, Cloud & Distributed Computing

Provides scalable infrastructure, edge computing, cloud services and distributed systems that make information and computing resources available where needed.

O

Observation, Sensing & Remote Intelligence

Uses connected sensors, imaging, satellite data and environmental monitoring to detect conditions, measure change and turn observations into actionable information.

P

Privacy, Protection & Personal Data

Builds privacy-preserving data practices, access controls, encryption, consent management and secure computation to protect people and sensitive information.

Q

Quantum Computing & Information

Researches quantum algorithms, quantum simulation, error mitigation and quantum information science for carefully selected problems in chemistry, materials, optimization and related fields.

R

Research, Science & Simulation

Accelerates scientific discovery through research platforms, modelling, simulation, reproducible workflows and collaboration across disciplines.

S

Security, Resilience & Risk

Designs resilient systems, threat monitoring, continuity planning, safety engineering and risk intelligence to keep critical information services dependable.

T

Transport, Time & Positioning

Supports navigation, timing, geospatial information, fleet coordination and intelligent transport. Quantum sensing may be explored for precision measurement where suitable.

U

User Experience & Human–Technology Interaction

Makes technology understandable, accessible and inclusive through human-centred design, assistive interfaces, multilingual services and usable information presentation.

V

Virtual Worlds, Visualisation & Digital Twins

Creates simulations, immersive environments, 3D visualisation and digital twins to help people inspect complex systems, train safely and test scenarios.

W

Water, Weather & Environment

Uses environmental data, forecasting, sensor networks and modelling to support water security, climate adaptation, disaster preparedness and sustainable resource management.

X

eXchange, Transactions & Cross-System Integration

Builds standards, APIs, data-exchange layers and transaction systems that allow organisations and technologies to share information reliably across boundaries.

Y

Youth, Society & Human Development

Develops inclusive digital services for communities, youth opportunity, employment pathways, civic participation and social development, with safeguards against exclusion and misuse.

Z

Zero-Friction Integration & Future Systems

Unifies the A–Y domains through common architecture, identity, interoperability, governance and measurement. It aims to make trusted information flow seamlessly across the ecosystem.
